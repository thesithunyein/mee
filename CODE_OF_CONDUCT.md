# Code of Conduct

Mee is built for people in Myanmar and anyone who studies fire and smoke. Contributors come from many places, speak different languages and have different levels of experience. We want everyone to feel welcome.

## Our pledge

We will make taking part in this project free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people writing in their second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and accept feedback with care.
- Focus on what helps the people who use Mee.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Using Mee's issues or discussions to promote violence or target any group

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), in the Telegram bot and channel, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub: https://github.com/thesithunyein/mee/security/advisories/new, or by direct message to [@thesithunyein](https://x.com/thesithunyein). All reports are read and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does so, depending on how serious it is and whether it repeats.
